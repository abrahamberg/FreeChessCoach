Free game review

Every move of your game, explained in words. Free.

Load a game from Chess.com or Lichess, or paste a PGN. Each move gets a verdict and a note that says what happened: what you missed, what you allowed and what the better move was. No account, and nothing is saved: the engine runs in your browser, or on our server when your device is slow.

Review my game, free

What you get

A note on every move, not just a number

  • A verdict on each move, from best to blunder, in the move list.
  • Notes in plain words at each move: the tactic you missed or allowed, and the move that was better.
  • A Game Report with accuracy and an estimated rating for both sides.
  • An evaluation graph that shows where the game turned.
  • Explore: try other moves on the board and see the engine's lines.

How it works

Review a game in three steps

  1. Open the free review

    Go to freechesscoach.org/review and choose where your game is: Chess.com, Lichess or Paste a PGN.

  2. Find your game

    Type your username and press Find my games. Your 20 most recent games appear. For an older game, or one from anywhere else, paste its PGN and say whether you played White or Black.

  3. Read the review

    Pick the game. The engine goes through it on your device (on our server, for a slow one), then the review opens from your side of the board. Step through the moves and read the note on each one.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is the game review really free?

Yes. The review at freechesscoach.org/review costs nothing and needs no account. There is no paid tier.

Do I need an account?

No. You can review a game without signing in. Sign in with Google only if you want your games saved, stats across all of them, the bots and the coach.

Is my game stored?

No. The engine runs in your browser; on a phone or a slower computer the positions are analyzed on our server instead and not stored. Leaving the page drops the review. Your Chess.com or Lichess username goes from your browser straight to that site to list your games.

Why does the first review take a while?

The chess engine, about 95 MB, downloads into your browser on the first visit and is cached after that. On a phone or a slower computer the review runs on our server instead, so nothing large is downloaded; you can switch either way while it runs.

Do I need an AI key?

No. The review's notes come from the chess engine and our own analysis code, with no AI service involved. An AI key is only for the coach, after you sign in.
