What you get

The review explains the move, not just the score

  • A verdict on each move, from best to blunder, in the move list.
  • Notes in plain words on every move: the tactic you missed or allowed, and the move that was better.
  • A Game Report with accuracy and an estimated rating for both sides.
  • An evaluation graph that shows where the game turned.
  • Explore: try other moves on the board and see the engine's lines.

How it works

Review a Chess.com game in three steps

  1. Open the free review and choose Chess.com

    Go to freechesscoach.org/review. Chess.com is already selected.

  2. Type your Chess.com username

    It is the end of your profile address (chess.com/member/username). Press Find my games and your 20 most recent games appear. You never enter a password. For an older game, choose Paste a PGN instead and say whether you played White or Black.

  3. Pick the game and read the review

    The engine goes through the game on your device (on our server, for a slow one), then the review opens from your side of the board. Step through the moves and read the note on each one.

Questions

Frequently asked

How many Game Reviews does a free Chess.com account get?

Chess.com's own help page lists "1 full game review analysis per day" for its free Basic membership, unlimited Game Review from Platinum, and coach explanations on all of your moves in Diamond (read on 12 November 2026). Check that page for the current terms.

Do I need to download my Chess.com games?

No. Type your Chess.com username and your 20 most recent games are listed. For an older game, paste its PGN instead.

Do I need to give my Chess.com password?

No. Only your public username. Your browser reads your public game list straight from Chess.com; the username never reaches our server.

Is there a daily limit?

The no-account review has no daily count and stores nothing: it runs on your device, or for a slow device on our server within an hourly allowance. Signed-in imports, which are saved to your library, are limited to 30 a day.

Does it work with Lichess games too?

Yes. Choose Lichess instead of Chess.com and type your Lichess username, or paste a PGN from anywhere.
