Bring your own key

Your AI key: how it works, and how to keep it safe.

The AI coach runs on a key you bring yourself. This page says plainly what we do with it, what we cannot promise, and how to set things up so that even the worst case is small. There is nothing here to panic about, and a few things worth doing.

The short version

Make a key just for FreeChessCoach, cap it at a small monthly amount (for example $5), and rotate it now and then. That is the whole safety plan. The rest of this page explains why.

Why we ask you to bring a key

Everything we build is free, but an AI answering you costs real money to run. Most products cover that with a subscription and add a mark-up. FreeChessCoach is a free project run by an individual, not a company, so it treats the AI like your internet connection: a separate service you choose yourself. Your key talks to your AI provider and you pay them directly at their price, which can be nothing if you pick a free plan. Nothing is added and nothing is metered by us.

The free features (import, stats, game review, bots) never touch a key. You only need one if you want the AI coach.

What we do to protect your key

It is stored encrypted, with a phrase only you know

When you save your setup you choose an unlock phrase (at least 8 characters). Your key is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from that phrase. The server has no master key for it, so a copy of our database alone cannot decrypt anyone's key, and the phrase itself is never stored.

Using the coach means unlocking it, briefly

When you enter your phrase, the decrypted setup is held in a short-lived cache on our server, encrypted again with a server-side key. By default it expires after 15 minutes without use, and each coaching request refreshes that clock. You can press Lock now in Settings at any time to remove it immediately.

You can delete it whenever you want

Delete setup in Settings removes the stored key. If you forget your phrase we cannot recover the key, and you simply connect a new one.

The key goes to your provider and nowhere else

Requests are sent to the AI endpoint you configure (OpenAI by default), under your own account with them, so you can see every call in your provider's usage page. If you choose a local model instead, there is no key and no provider: requests go from your browser tab to the server on your own computer.

You can check all of this yourself

The code is open source. The encryption lives in key-vault.ts and the unlock cache in unlock-store.ts. Please read them and decide for yourself.

What we cannot promise

Encryption protects a stolen database. It cannot protect a key that is in use.

While your setup is unlocked (up to 15 minutes after your last coaching request), a decrypted copy exists in our server's cache. If someone broke into the running system at that moment and also got the server's own secrets, they could read the keys of people who were unlocked then.

We have done our best to make that hard, and it is the reason we ask you to use a limited key. With a small monthly cap, a leaked key is worth at most that cap, and you can revoke it in seconds.

We are also not a company, and this is a free project. We can't accept liability for charges, losses or damages, so please use the coach at your own risk and read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. We would rather tell you this now than have you find out later.

Your safety checklist

Five small habits. Together they turn "what if" into "at most a few dollars".

  1. Make a key only for this app

    Create a new key (in its own project, if your provider supports projects) and use it nowhere else. If anything ever looks wrong, you can delete it without breaking anything else you run.

  2. Put a small monthly limit on it

    Start with about $5 a month. Use the coach for a couple of weeks, look at your provider's usage page, and adjust the limit to what you really use. Keep it tight rather than generous.

  3. Rotate it from time to time

    Every month or two, create a fresh key, replace it in Settings, and delete the old one in your provider's dashboard. A key that keeps changing is a key that is hard to misuse.

  4. Lock it when you are done, if you like

    Press Lock now after a session and the decrypted copy is gone from our server straight away. If you prefer, delete the setup entirely after every session and paste it again next time.

  5. Choose a strong unlock phrase you do not reuse

    The phrase is what protects the stored key. Use something long that you don't use anywhere else.

Questions

Can I use a provider other than OpenAI?

The setup form accepts an endpoint that speaks the OpenAI Chat or Responses format, or Anthropic's Messages format, and it tests the connection before saving. The step-by-step guide covers OpenAI because it is the default.

Can I run the coach on a local model?

Yes. In Settings → AI setup choose On my computer (LM Studio / Ollama). Calls are relayed through your open browser tab to the server on your machine, so keep the tab open while you coach, and the local server must allow this site (CORS). Small models can be slower or less reliable than a cloud one. See the local setup steps.

What happens when my monthly limit runs out?

Your provider stops answering requests, so the coach can't respond. Nothing is lost. Raise the limit in your provider's dashboard, or wait for the next month. The free features keep working.

Does the coach see my Google account or password?

No. Google only tells us your email address and name when you sign in, and we never see your password. Your AI provider sees only what the coach sends it: the game and conversation it is working on.

What if I think my key was exposed?

Delete it in your provider's dashboard first, since that stops any use at once. Then press Delete setup in FreeChessCoach and connect a new key.
