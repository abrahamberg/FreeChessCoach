Privacy Policy

Effective August 24, 2026

FreeChessCoach ("FreeChessCoach", "we", "us") is an independent, individually operated project — there is no company behind it. This policy explains what data the service collects when you use it, why, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, email hello@freechesscoach.org and ask.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and the display name Google provides. We don't see or store your Google password.
  • Profile information you provide. Rating band, Lichess/Chess.com usernames, free-text self-assessment notes, and coaching preferences (coach persona, voice/TTS settings) you enter in Settings.
  • Game data. The games you import — via Lichess, pasted PGN, or file upload — including move data, results, and any player names embedded in the PGN. If you play against other people, their names may appear in your imported games too.
  • Coaching sessions. The messages, analysis, and progress data generated as you work through a game with the coach, so it can pick up where you left off next time.
  • Your AI provider API key. FreeChessCoach is bring-your-own-key (BYOK) only — you connect your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key, either by pasting a key directly or by connecting through a supported platform integration. The key is encrypted at rest with a passphrase only you know. We don't generate, hold, or have access to that passphrase — it's never stored or logged anywhere. Practically, this means your key is unreadable to us by default: we can decrypt and use it only for the moment you supply the passphrase to start or resume a coaching session, and we discard the decrypted key from memory as soon as that session's calls are done. If you forget your passphrase, we cannot recover your key for you — you'll need to remove it and connect it again.

How we use it

To run the service: authenticate you, analyze and store your games, generate coaching responses, and track your progress across sessions. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising. FreeChessCoach has no paid features, credits, or purchases of any kind — nothing you do here involves paying us.

Who your data is shared with

FreeChessCoach's whole model is that AI calls happen using your own credentials, which you can trace. Depending on how you use the service, your data passes through:

  • Google — for sign-in (OAuth). Google's own privacy policy governs what Google does with your authentication activity.
  • Anthropic and/or OpenAI, or the platform you've connected — to generate coaching responses and analysis. Once you've supplied your passphrase for a session, the relevant game and session content is sent directly to that provider using your own key, under your account with them, and their terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.
  • OpenAI — separately, if you choose the higher-quality synthesized coach voice, using your own connected key. The default voice engine (Kokoro) runs entirely in your browser instead, and never leaves your device.
  • Our hosting and infrastructure providers, to the extent needed to run servers and a database in the ordinary course of operating the service.

We do not sell or rent your information to anyone, and we don't share it with third parties beyond what's needed to provide the service described above.

Cookies

FreeChessCoach doesn't set its own tracking or analytics cookies. Staying signed in relies on a session cookie set by our authentication layer (Google OAuth) — necessary for the service to work, not used for tracking across other sites.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account, games, and session data for as long as your account exists, so your coaching history and progress stay available to you. There's currently no self-serve "delete my account" button — email hello@freechesscoach.org from the address on your account and we'll delete your account and associated data by hand. Deleting a connected API key from Settings removes it immediately and permanently.

Security

We take reasonable, standard precautions: encryption in transit, passphrase-based encryption at rest for API keys (so the key is unusable without you), and access controls around the database. That said, no online service can be guaranteed perfectly secure, and we don't claim FreeChessCoach is immune to bugs, misconfigurations, or attacks by third parties. See the Terms of Service for how that risk is allocated.

Children's privacy

FreeChessCoach is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll remove it.

International use

FreeChessCoach is operated from Sweden and processed on servers and by the third-party providers listed above, which may be located in countries other than your own. By using the service, you understand your information may be processed outside your country of residence.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the effective date above. Continuing to use FreeChessCoach after a change means you accept the update.
