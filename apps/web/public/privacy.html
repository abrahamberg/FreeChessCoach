Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

FreeChessCoach ("FreeChessCoach", "we", "us") is an independent, individually operated project — there is no company behind it. This policy explains what data the service collects when you use it, why, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, email hello@freechesscoach.org and ask.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and the display name Google provides. We don't see or store your Google password. The full detail is in "Google sign-in data" below.
  • Profile information you provide. Rating band, Lichess/Chess.com usernames, free-text self-assessment notes, and coaching preferences (coach persona, voice/TTS settings) you enter in Settings.
  • Game data. The games you import — via Lichess, pasted PGN, or a PGN file opened in your browser (only its text is sent) — including move data, results, and any player names embedded in the PGN. If you play against other people, their names may appear in your imported games too.
  • Coaching sessions. The messages, analysis, and progress data generated as you work through a game with the coach, so it can pick up where you left off next time.
  • Your AI provider API key. FreeChessCoach is bring-your-own-AI — you connect your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key, or a model running on your own computer (LM Studio or Ollama, which needs no key). For an API key, you connect it either by pasting a key directly or by connecting through a supported platform integration. The key is encrypted at rest with a passphrase only you know. We don't generate, hold, or have access to that passphrase — it's never stored or logged anywhere. Practically, this means your key is unreadable to us by default: a copy of our database alone cannot decrypt it. When you supply the passphrase to start or resume a coaching session, the decrypted key is held for a short time in a cache on our server, encrypted again with a server-side key. It expires after a period without use (15 minutes by default, and each coaching request restarts the clock), and you can remove it at once with "Lock now" in Settings. While it is unlocked, the server can use it to make your coaching calls. If you forget your passphrase, we cannot recover your key for you — you'll need to remove it and connect it again.

Google sign-in data

We use Google for one thing only: signing you in. We ask Google for the minimum needed to create your account — the email address on your Google account and the display name on your Google profile — and nothing else. We don't request or receive access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, photos, calendar, or any other part of your Google account.

  • What we use it for. Creating your account, recognizing you while you're signed in, showing your display name, and emailing you about your account. Nothing else — no advertising, no profiling, and no other use of your Google identity anywhere in the product.
  • How it's stored. Your email address and display name are stored in our database for as long as your account exists. The sign-in session that keeps you logged in is an encrypted cookie managed by our authentication layer and is used only to keep you logged in.
  • Who it's shared with. Nobody, note that the service providers might who process it only to run the service for us under strict terms and conditions.
  • How it's deleted. When your account is deleted (see "Data retention and deletion" below), the email address and name we received from Google are deleted with it. (your account might remain in our backup database for while until the backup is depricated)

Google's own handling of your sign-in activity is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

How we use it

To run the service: authenticate you, analyze and store your games, generate coaching responses, and track your progress across sessions. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising. FreeChessCoach has no paid features or purchases of any kind — nothing you do here involves paying us.

Who your data is shared with

FreeChessCoach's whole model is that AI calls happen using your own credentials, which you can trace. Depending on how you use the service, your data passes through:

  • Google — for sign-in (OAuth). Google's own privacy policy governs what Google does with your authentication activity.
  • Anthropic and/or OpenAI, or the platform you've connected (not applicable if you use a local model, whose requests go from your browser to your own machine) — to generate coaching responses and analysis. Once you've supplied your passphrase for a session, the relevant game and session content is sent directly to that provider using your own key, under your account with them, and their terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.
  • OpenAI — separately, if you choose the higher-quality synthesized coach voice, using your own connected key. The default voice engine (Kokoro) runs entirely in your browser instead, and never leaves your device.
  • Our hosting and infrastructure providers, to the extent needed to run servers and a database in the ordinary course of operating the service.

We do not sell or rent your information to anyone, and we don't share it with third parties beyond what's needed to provide the service described above.

Cookies

FreeChessCoach doesn't set its own tracking or analytics cookies. Staying signed in relies on a session cookie set by our authentication layer (Google OAuth) — necessary for the service to work, not used for tracking across other sites.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account, games, and session data for as long as your account exists, so your coaching history and progress stay available to you. There's currently no self-serve "delete my account" button — email hello@freechesscoach.org from the address on your account and we'll delete your account and associated data by hand. Deleting a connected API key from Settings removes it immediately and permanently.

Security

We take reasonable, standard precautions: encryption in transit, passphrase-based encryption at rest for API keys (so a stolen copy of the database alone cannot use them), and access controls around the database. That said, no online service can be guaranteed perfectly secure, and we don't claim FreeChessCoach is immune to bugs, misconfigurations, or attacks by third parties. See the Terms of Service for how that risk is allocated.

Children's privacy

FreeChessCoach is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll remove it.

International use

FreeChessCoach is operated from Sweden and processed on servers and by the third-party providers listed above, which may be located in countries other than your own. By using the service, you understand your information may be processed outside your country of residence.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the effective date above. Continuing to use FreeChessCoach after a change means you accept the update.
