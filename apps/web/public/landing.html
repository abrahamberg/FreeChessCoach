What is in it for us?

Nothing, on purpose.

You may be wondering what the catch is. There isn't one. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and from Google we only ask for your email and name.

We love chess, and we believe everyone deserves free access to digital coaching. Lichess does a wonderful job giving the world a free place to play and learn. What was missing was a tool that reads your games and tells you why you keep losing them, so we built that. Human coaches do wonderful work and always will; this doesn't replace them, it just means good feedback on your own games doesn't depend on what you can pay for.

Chess belongs to everyone.

Chess began in India and grew up in Persia, today's Iran, before it travelled the world. Even the word "checkmate" comes from Persian. A game that old is part of human heritage, and we believe the game itself should stay open to everyone.

Keeping digital chess coaching free, and belonging to everyone, for as many people as we can reach: that is the whole mission.

Why it is built this way

"You forgot to check for threats" is not a diagnosis.

We asked our coach whether "Fails to run CCT" (checks, captures, threats) is a good diagnosis.

Is "Fails to run CCT" a good diagnosis?

No. It is a terrible diagnosis.

"Failing to run CCT" is not a diagnosis; it is a symptom. It is the coaching equivalent of a doctor saying, "My diagnosis is that you failed to stay healthy."

Telling a student "You need to check for Checks, Captures, and Threats" is useless because every 800-rated player already knows they should do it. They aren't failing because they lack the knowledge of CCT; they are failing because a psychological or mechanical barrier prevents them from executing it.

From an interview with our coach

So FreeChessCoach looks for the mechanism behind a mistake: a habit of planning around the board as it was one move ago, or of never checking where a knight can jump. Then it gives you one thing to practise.

Quick answers

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything from us is free: no subscription, no paid tier, no credit card. The coach's AI is a separate thing that you connect yourself, like your own internet subscription. What it costs, if anything, is between you and the provider. Some have free plans, and on a paid key you can set a monthly spending limit.

Do I need an AI to use it?

Only for the coach. Importing, stats, game review and the bots work without one. To chat with the coach you connect an AI of your choice: a cloud service with your own key, or a model running on your own computer (LM Studio or Ollama). We are not in that business and add nothing on top. Here is one way to set it up, including how some free usage works and what it costs in privacy, or run it locally for free and private.

Is it safe to paste my key?

We encrypt it with a phrase only you know, and we ask you to use a key with a small monthly limit. The key page explains what we do, what we cannot promise, and how to set up a limited key in a few minutes.

Can I look around without signing in?

Yes. The tour shows every screen with sample data, and the guide walks through a first session.

Who is behind this, and what do they get?

One person, not a company, and nothing in return: no ads, no data sales. The AI is bring-your-own so the project can stay free for everyone without a business model that needs you to keep paying. Here is why we do it. The code is open source.
