What makes it a coach

“You forgot to check for threats” is not a diagnosis.

Every player already knows to look for checks, captures and threats. Being told again is like a doctor diagnosing you with “failed to stay healthy”. FreeChessCoach looks across your games for the reason you keep missing things, then gives you one thing to practise.

  • The engine

    37…Ng5 was a blunder. 37…Rxd2+ would have kept you ahead.

  • Most feedback

    Always check for checks, captures and threats.

  • Your coach

    “The same thing twice: the board changed and your picture of it did not.”

    Homework: after every capture, ask what it changed. Then look for the recapture first.

What is in it for us?

Nothing, on purpose.

We love chess, and we think good feedback on your own games shouldn't depend on what you can pay. Lichess gives the world a free place to play; FreeChessCoach adds a coach that reads your games. Human coaches do wonderful work, and this doesn't replace them.

Chess began in India and grew up in Persia, today's Iran; even the word “checkmate” comes from Persian. A game that old is part of human heritage, and learning it should stay open to everyone.

Quick answers

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything from us is free: no subscription, no paid tier, no credit card. Stats, game review, the bots and the courses need no AI at all. The coach's AI is a separate thing you connect yourself, like your own internet plan, so what it costs, if anything, is between you and the provider. Some have free tiers, a local model costs nothing, and on a paid key you can set a monthly spending limit.

Do I need an AI to use it?

Only for the coach. Importing, stats, game review, the bots and the courses work without one. To chat with the coach you connect an AI of your choice: a cloud service with your own key, or a model running on your own computer (LM Studio or Ollama). We are not in that business and add nothing on top. The setup guide walks through a cloud key step by step, or you can run it locally, free and private.

Is it safe to paste my key?

We encrypt it with a phrase only you know, and we ask you to use a key with a small monthly limit. The key page explains what we do, what we cannot promise, and how to set up a limited key in a few minutes.

Can I look around without signing in?

Yes. The free game review needs no account: pick a Chess.com or Lichess game, or paste a PGN. The free courses need no sign-in at all. The live demo is the real app on sample data, coaching session included. The tour shows every screen, and the guide walks through a first session.

Who is behind this, and what do they get?

One person, not a company, and nothing in return: no ads, no data sales. The AI is bring-your-own so the project can stay free for everyone without a business model that needs you to keep paying. Here is why we do it. The code is public on GitHub.
